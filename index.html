Grammar I: Can and Can't for Ability

We use can to talk about abilities (things animals or people are able to do) and can't (cannot) for things they are not able to do.

Affirmative (+) & Negative (-)

  • A penguin can swim.
  • It can't fly.
  • Penguins can swim. They can't fly.

Note: can't = can not

Yes/No Questions & Answers

  • Can a penguin swim? → Yes, it can. / No, it can't.
  • Can penguins swim? → Yes, they can. / No, they can't.

Grammar II: Simple Present - Yes/No Questions with Have

We use Do or Does to ask questions about physical features and possession using have.

Singular (It / Animals)

  • Does a panda have a short tail?
  • Yes, it does. / No, it doesn't.

Note: doesn't = does not

Plural (Pandas / They)

  • Do pandas have long necks?
  • Yes, they do. / No, they don't.

Note: don't = do not

💡 Resolved Examples (15 Levels)

Review how sentences and questions are structured across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels.

🌱 Basic Level Examples (Can / Can't & Has)

Basic #1

Sentence with can:

A fish can swim.

Basic #2

Sentence with can't:

A rabbit can't fly.

Basic #3

Question with Can:

Can a bird fly? → Yes, it can.

Basic #4

Question with Does... have:

Does a cat have a tail? → Yes, it does.

Basic #5

Question with Do... have:

Do dogs have four legs? → Yes, they do.

⚡ Intermediate Level Examples (Combining Abilities & Negative Answers)

Intermediate #6

Negative ability short answer:

Can a hippo climb? → No, it can't.

Intermediate #7

Plural ability sentence:

Monkeys can climb, but they can't swim.

Intermediate #8

Negative possessive answer (singular):

Does a monkey have wings? → No, it doesn't.

Intermediate #9

Negative possessive answer (plural):

Do crocodiles have long necks? → No, they don't.

Intermediate #10

Multi-action contrast (Sonia):

Sonia can play basketball, but she can't cook.

🔥 Advanced Level Examples (Complex Multi-clause & Animal Comparisons)

Advanced #11

Double ability contrast with personal pronouns:

Kangaroos can hop and run, but they can't climb or fly.

Advanced #12

Comparative question formulation:

Does an elephant have a long trunk and big ears? → Yes, it does.

Advanced #13

Family member skill contrast (Brother):

Her brother can play soccer, but he can't climb a tree.

Advanced #14

Plural animal feature interrogation:

Do giraffes have long necks and short tails? → Yes, they do.

Advanced #15

Personal ability statement (Unit 3 application):

I can ride a bike, but I can't drive a car.

🤔 Critical Reflection Questions

Open questions for conceptual analysis and logical thinking in Second Grade.

Question 1: Animal Abilities

Why do you think animals like fish can swim, but land animals like hippos cannot? How does their body help them?

Question 2: Rules of Grammar

When do we use Does instead of Do when asking questions about animals?

Question 3: Personal Growth

Think of something you couldn't do when you were a baby, but you can do now. Why is practice important?

Question 4: Teamwork & Nature

If animals have different abilities (running, climbing, swimming), how do they help each other live in nature?
